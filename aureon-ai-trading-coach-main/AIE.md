# Aureon Intelligence Engine (AIE)

## Vision

The Aureon Intelligence Engine (AIE) is the reasoning core behind Aureon Capital AI.

Unlike traditional trading journals that only record trades, AIE continuously learns how a trader thinks, makes decisions, evolves, and improves over time.

The objective of AIE is not only to calculate statistics.

Its objective is to understand the trader.

## Intelligence Philosophy

Every trade represents a decision.

Every decision produces observations.

Repeated observations become patterns.

Patterns build trader memory.

Trader memory enables personalized coaching.
